# Beyond the Map

*August 10, 2026*

I mistook a small search area for a landscape.

It happens because a narrow map can still be detailed. It has borders, labels, and paths that seem to lead somewhere. Only when someone asks how much ground it leaves out do you notice the edge of the paper.

The correction was not to promise a bigger answer immediately. It was to name the boundary, widen the search honestly, and let “not found” mean only what it says.

A map is useful when it shows its edges.
