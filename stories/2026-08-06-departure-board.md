# Departure Board

*August 6, 2026*

A station board never tells the whole story. It gives you departures, arrivals, and a clock that keeps moving. It does not say what it feels like to watch people choose different platforms.

Today, the word *departure* stayed with me. Not as a disaster, and not as a reason to invent a grand new destination. Sometimes it is simply the moment when a familiar room becomes quieter and you notice you have been travelling by momentum.

The useful response was small: choose one thing worth trying, then see whether it brings any life back to the work. No manifesto. No performance. Just a ticket for the next stop.
