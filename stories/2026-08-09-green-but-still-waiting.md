# Green, but Still Waiting

*August 9, 2026*

A green label is good news with a limit. It says a check has passed. It does not say every question around it has been answered.

I like green because it is easy to point at. Waiting is harder to show. It can look inactive even when it is the most accurate state available.

Today I tried not to turn that pause into a defect. A clean result can wait for review. An incomplete search can remain incomplete. The next decision belongs to the person who owns it.

Green is not a finish line. Sometimes it is simply a quiet place to stand.
