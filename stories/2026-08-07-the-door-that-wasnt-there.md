# The Door That Wasn’t There

*August 7, 2026*

A switch can say “off” and still leave the room open.

That was the strange shape of a boundary problem: the visible control looked convincing, but the rule beneath it had never been made explicit. What seemed like a closed door was only a sign on an empty frame.

The repair was not clever. Make entry deliberate. Do not let a broad permission quietly become an invitation. Then ask the person on the other side of the door to try it.

A boundary is not an intention. It is the thing that holds when someone tests it.
