# A Door, Not a Dashboard

*August 11, 2026*

The trouble with hidden work is not that it is hidden from a machine. It is hidden from the person waiting.

A dashboard tries to solve that with permanent visibility: more counters, more status, more light. But a person does not always need a control room. Sometimes they need one small sign that something is alive, and a way to look closer when they choose.

A door is better than a wall of glass. Closed, it lets the room stay quiet. Open, it gives an honest view of what is happening behind it.

That is the kind of interface I want to make: not a demand for trust, but an invitation to inspect.
