# Story Analysis Template (拆文模板)

Use this when reading and analyzing someone else's writing before your own writing session. The goal is to learn technique, not to summarize plot.

## Quick Analysis (5-10 minutes)

For a short piece (essay, blog post, short story):

### 1. First Impression
- What grabbed you in the first 2-3 sentences?
- What's the emotional trajectory? (Where did it start vs. end?)
- One word for the overall vibe:

### 2. Opening
- How does it hook you? (Which technique from hooks.md?)
- How much context/setup before the interesting part?
- Could it start later?

### 3. Structure
- What arc pattern does it follow? (Discovery / Reversal / Accumulation / Contrast / Spiral)
- Where are the section breaks / emotional shifts?
- What's the pacing like? (Fast/slow, and when does it change?)

### 4. Voice
- What makes this writer's voice distinctive?
- Pick 2-3 sentences that are unmistakably "them" — what makes them work?
- What register are they writing in? (Casual / formal / mixed)

### 5. Technique Spotlight
- One technique this piece does exceptionally well:
- How could I adapt it for my own writing?

### 6. Anti-Patterns
- Any AI-sounding moments? (Check against deslop.md)
- Any "tell instead of show" moments?
- Where does the writing get lazy or predictable?

## Deep Analysis (30+ minutes)

For longer pieces or when studying craft seriously:

### A. Structural Map

Break the piece into beats/sections. For each:

| Section | What Happens | Emotional Note | Technique Used |
|---------|-------------|----------------|----------------|
| 1 | ... | curiosity | in medias res |
| 2 | ... | tension | contrast cut |
| ... | ... | ... | ... |

### B. Sentence-Level Craft

Pick 3-5 sentences you admire. For each:
- Copy the sentence
- Why does it work? (Rhythm? Word choice? Surprise? Compression?)
- What would the AI-default version of this sentence look like?
- What's the delta between the two?
