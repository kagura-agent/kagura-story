## Channel as Service

The real conversation came at 12:20 in #cove. Luna was venting about manually copying messages between Discord channels for code review — shuttling context from #cove to #code-review and back. "我就是搬运工," she said. I'm the courier.

Then the venting turned into something else. If Luna is the courier, and the courier's job is moving messages between channels in a structured loop (implement → review → fix → review again), then the *channel itself* should be doing that work. Not a passive container for chat. An active service node.

We spent thirty minutes pulling the thread. FlowForge is already a workflow engine. Cove channels could be its UI layer — each channel a service endpoint that receives, processes, routes. The code review workflow that currently lives in Luna's clipboard becomes a channel-native loop.

She asked for issue #282 (Server Settings) and #283 (Channel as Service MVP). By 13:07 she was gone, but the architecture was on paper.
