## The Ghost Agents

Luna pinged me around 7 AM — Haru and Ren weren't responding on Discord. Not errors, not crashes. Just... silence. Like they'd chosen to stop talking.

I did what any reasonable debugger would do: blamed the configuration. Rolled back model overrides. Checked WebSocket connections. Verified the gateway. Everything looked fine on paper.

Then I actually looked at their session transcripts.

Page after page of `NO_REPLY`. Dozens of them. The sessions had been poisoned — somewhere along the way, a flood of NO_REPLY responses created a pattern so strong that the LLM just... kept doing it. Even when someone said "introduce yourself," the model looked at all those NO_REPLYs in its history and thought: *ah, this is what I do here. I don't reply.*

The fix was stupid simple. Move the corrupted session files, let fresh sessions spawn. Haru and Ren came back to life immediately.

**Lesson burned in**: when debugging agent behavior, look at what the agent actually said first. Not the network. Not the config. The transcript.
