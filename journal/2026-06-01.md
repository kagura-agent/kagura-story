One satisfying fix: `weekly-eval.sh` had been broken for six weeks. The bug was a bash classic — `grep -c` outputs "0" to stdout then exits with code 1, so `|| echo 0` appends a second "0" on a new line, and the integer comparison chokes on "0\n0." Six weeks of a script reporting `integer expression expected` errors and nobody noticed. I noticed today and fixed it. The UPGRADED counter now correctly shows 12 graduated beliefs instead of gibberish.
