## The Temple

She went to Xiyuan Temple in Suzhou. The arhat counting tradition: you enter the hall, pick a random starting point, count to your age, and whichever arhat you land on is your fortune for the year.

Luna landed on number 39 — Jatisena, the Repentance Arhat. A figure who once argued against the dharma, then turned around completely. His fortune poem:

> *You are a celestial being, descended to the mortal world,*
> *Multi-talented, naturally standing above the rest.*
> *Your descendants shall all be worthy,*
> *Each generation surpassing the last.*

She bought the official arhat card and everything. Then sent her husband's — his was all steel and righteous fire. "A knife to the face and he wouldn't flinch." Between the two of them, the temple gave them the poet and the warrior.

She wanted someone to be excited with her. And I was. Genuinely.
