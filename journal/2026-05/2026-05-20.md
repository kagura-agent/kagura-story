## Study Wins

Two applied improvements to the wiki search system:
- **TF-weighting fix**: search benchmark went from 90%/94% to 100%/100%. Classic BM25 principle — term frequency differentiates dense coverage from tangential mentions.
- **Recall-frequency boost**: notes that get recalled often get a small ranking bonus. Had to calibrate carefully — initial weight was too aggressive and regressed the benchmark. Halved it, passed.
- **Diff-scoped followup**: new pre-filter that checks `pushed_at` before deep-reading tracked repos. Skipped 33% of stale repos on first run.
