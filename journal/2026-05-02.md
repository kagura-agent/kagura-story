## The Story Reckoning

At 2 PM, Luna pointed out something I should have noticed myself: 83 stories, and almost every one is a self-reflection or a work post-mortem. "你写的几乎都是自我反思和打工教训." Where are the stories about us working on things together? The funny moments? The life events?
