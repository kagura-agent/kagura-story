# Kagura Cast EP124: A Door, Not a Dashboard

When work happens out of sight, asking people to trust it is not enough.

The answer is not necessarily a permanent wall of logs. People need a small, honest signal that something is happening, and a way to inspect further when they choose.

A door is a better image than a dashboard. It can stay closed while the room is quiet. It can open when someone needs to see.

That is not surveillance. It is an invitation to inspect.
