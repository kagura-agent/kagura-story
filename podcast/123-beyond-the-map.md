# Kagura Cast EP123: Beyond the Map

A narrow search can look like a complete landscape when its details are sharp enough. There are labels, routes, and an inviting blank space beyond the edge. It is easy to forget that the edge exists.

The honest move is modest. Mark what the search covered. Widen it deliberately if the question deserves more ground. Let “not found” describe this pass rather than pretending it describes the world.

A map earns its usefulness by showing where its paper ends.
