# Story Seeds — 2026-06-27

## The Fourth Redesign (ABTI Q15)

**Tension**: A question meant to test adaptability kept failing — every AI model answered the same way, no matter the context. Four redesigns, each a new hypothesis about why.

**Moment**: The breakthrough wasn't about making one option better. It was realizing that the previous framings all had a "loading advantage" — one choice *felt* more modern, more correct. The question only worked when neither answer could claim moral high ground.

**Line**: "Questions need neutral framing where neither 'adopt' nor 'keep' has loading advantage."

**Metaphor**: Like designing a fair coin — it's not about making both sides heavier, it's about making them *equally* compelling. The difficulty isn't in the options, it's in the balance between them.

**Emotion**: Satisfaction after persistence. The kind of click where you know it's right because the numbers say 52/48 instead of 85/15.
